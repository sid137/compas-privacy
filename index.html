Stepfolio privacy policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

Stepfolio is an app for filming and studying dance moves, made by an independent developer. This page says what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version: Stepfolio has no account, no server and no tracking. What you film and write stays on your iPhone. A few things leave your iPhone, and only when you do something that needs them. They're listed below.

What Stepfolio keeps on your iPhone

  • Your videos and photos. Videos you film or cut in Stepfolio are kept in the app's own files, and a copy is saved in Photos. Videos you add from Photos stay in Photos. Stepfolio points to them there, and makes a copy in its own files only when it needs one, for example to play a video or export your library.
  • What you write. Names of videos and steps, tips, notes, teachers, classes, places and tags are kept in the app's database on your iPhone.
  • What you say for a tip. When you tap "Speak a tip" and talk, Stepfolio keeps the sound in its own files on your iPhone, as you said it. It records only after that tap, and keeps the sound even if you don't add the tip.
  • Where a video was filmed. When you add videos from Photos, Stepfolio reads the place stored in each video, to match it to your class. That place stays on your iPhone. Stepfolio never asks where your iPhone is.
  • The app's log. This is a short record of what the app did, for example a copy that failed. It holds the last 500 entries, with times, technical messages, the app's own file names and Photos' identifiers for videos. Sometimes it also holds the address of a link you added. It doesn't hold the names of your videos, teachers or places, or what you say. It never leaves your iPhone unless you send it in a problem report.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud, Apple's backup includes what Stepfolio keeps, your voice tips too. That backup is between you and Apple.

What Stepfolio never does

  • It has no account and no sign-in.
  • It has no server. Nothing you film or write is sent to the developer.
  • It has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
  • It doesn't sell your information. The developer never receives it, unless you send a problem report.
  • It never asks for your iPhone's location.
  • It never turns what you say into text off your iPhone. Speech recognition is set to run on the iPhone only.
  • It never sends your voice to any service. A voice tip stays on your iPhone, in your own export when you make one, and in your iPhone's backup.
  • It never deletes anything from Photos.

Your choices

  • Permissions. Stepfolio asks before it uses each of these, and you can change your answer anytime in iPhone Settings:

    PermissionWhat it's for
    Camera"Lets you record videos."
    Microphone"Records the sound of your videos and what you say while you film. Tips you speak stay on your iPhone, as you said them."
    Speech recognition"Turns what you say into steps and tips."
    Photos"Shows the videos in Photos so you can add them."
    Adding to Photos"Saves a copy in Photos of each video you film or cut."

    Without a permission, the feature that needs it doesn't work, and the rest of the app still does.

  • Links. If you don't want a service to see a video's address, don't add links from it.

  • Problem reports. Sending one is up to you. You can read the email and its attachment before you send it.

  • Your data. You can export your library at any time from Settings. Deleting Stepfolio from your iPhone deletes what it keeps. Your videos in Photos stay.
